AI has deeply fascinated me. It is not intelligent per se, nor is it sentinent. But what interests me are the effects it will have on society and humanity as a whole. Will humans go back to seeing each other in person? Will we eradicate work or tedious tasks? Will we become more caring, compassionate or happy? I don't know. But what I will tell you for certain is we are living through once in a lifetime opportunity that will shape the course of humanity for centuries to come.

First AI as chatbots lack the constant feedback loop that humans have. We can intake all form of sensory data and our minds are constantly filtering out what's important and what's not. Chatbots only take a small fraction of inputs that the human had to deliberatly output. For example, when I give the chatbot instructions on how to write a software program, the AI has only my "approved" input to base the output from. In other words, chatbots cannot feel or understand what it means to be a human. Physical AI (robotics) will be the next obvious path in developing feedback loops and giving AI the ability to "understand" and "experience" the world around it. 

As these feedback loops continue to decrease in latency and increase in data, humanity will need to transition to some other economic or social system to meet the demands of its citizens. As physical AI continues to improve it will subsequently improve the software AI causing a domino effect that might lead to the end of humanity (whether it be the long way or the short way). In other words, consciousness will expand in the universe, but without us. That is the most likely path assuming developments in software and physical AI continue. I don't want to go further into this timeline because all of it has been covered in greater detail on Space & Stuff. 

The long term path for humanity is dark, and there is pretty much nothing we can do to stop it. Humans are not capable off surviving anywhere beyond the Earth's surface, we are plagued by greed, corruption, sin and war. However, I do believe that the future short term timeline will present humanities with endless opportunity and age of abundance. I am listing some predictions that I believe will occur to humanity in the next couple of years, depending on the pace in advancements of software AI and its corresponding mate, physical AI. 

The first prediction is that operating systems as we know it today would become a single prompt, where anyone will be able to do anything from it. Instead of having apps, folders, or apps for folders (as in Finder for Mac users), we will have a single search bar, one that is always present and constantly visible. From that, we will be able to invoke any command, whether it is opening a new website, or changing certain settings. You might ask it to show a microphone option at all times to allow you to give verbal directions, or tell it to change the font of the text after 9PM each day. In other words, one medium of communication (in this case, text) is all you need to be able to do anything you want on your laptop or desktop.
